01 / Privacy

Privacy Policy

Four52 may collect personal information when a person uses the website or chooses to interact with Four52 services. The exact information collected depends on the service being used.

Information may include

  • Name, email address, telephone number and account details.
  • Artist, creator, promoter or community profile information submitted by the user.
  • Booking, event, ticket or merchandise order information.
  • Messages, enquiries, reports and support requests.
  • Content intentionally uploaded or submitted to the platform.
  • Technical information such as device, browser, session, security and website-usage data where applicable.

Why information may be used

  • To operate accounts, profiles and website features.
  • To process enquiries, bookings, tickets, purchases and support requests.
  • To secure the platform and prevent abuse or fraud.
  • To communicate service information and, where permitted, marketing messages.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

Personal information should only be kept for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to legal, contractual, security and record-keeping requirements.

02 / Data protection

POPIA & Your Rights

Four52 operates from South Africa and intends to process personal information in line with applicable South African data-protection requirements, including the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), where it applies.

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, a person may request to:

  • Ask whether Four52 holds personal information about them.
  • Request access to eligible personal information.
  • Correct or update inaccurate information.
  • Object to certain processing.
  • Request deletion where there is a lawful basis for deletion.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Opt out of eligible direct marketing.
Privacy requests: Requests may require reasonable identity verification before account or personal information is disclosed, changed or deleted.

03 / Platform rules

Terms of Use

By using Four52 website features or services, users are responsible for using them lawfully and in accordance with the rules that apply to the relevant service.

Users must not

  • Use another person's identity or account without permission.
  • Use the service to commit fraud, scams or unlawful activity.
  • Attempt to interfere with the security or operation of the website.
  • Upload content they do not own or have permission to use.
  • Harass, threaten, exploit or deliberately harm other users.

Four52 may restrict, suspend or terminate access to features where reasonably necessary for security, rule enforcement, legal compliance or protection of the platform and its users.

Cookie Policy

The website may use cookies or similar technologies for essential functions such as security, sessions, preferences, performance and analytics. Optional advertising or marketing technologies should only be used in accordance with the consent and legal requirements that apply to them.

Cookie categories

Analytics: help understand website usage and performance where enabled.

05 / Events

Tickets & Events

Specific ticket terms may apply to each event. Before purchasing, customers should review the event date, venue, ticket type, age restrictions, entry requirements, refund conditions and any venue-specific rules.

  • Tickets may be subject to identity, scanning or fraud-verification checks.
  • Cancelled or postponed events may have event-specific refund or rescheduling procedures.
  • Unauthorised duplication, alteration or fraudulent resale of tickets may invalidate entry.
  • Venue safety rules and lawful instructions must be followed.

07 / Community

Community Guidelines

Four52 community features are intended for artists, creators, fans, promoters and collaborators to connect without harassment, fraud or deliberate abuse.

Prohibited conduct may include

  • Harassment, threats or hateful abuse.
  • Impersonation, fraud, scams or deceptive activity.
  • Spam, malicious links or platform manipulation.
  • Illegal content or unauthorised disclosure of private information.
  • Copyright or intellectual-property infringement.

Possible enforcement may include content removal, feature restrictions, temporary suspension or permanent account removal depending on the circumstances.
